# Offer Positioning & Sales Copy Prompts

A collection of AI prompts designed for crafting high-converting sales page copy, clear value propositions, audience-focused problem sections, and sharp positioning statements.

---

## 01. Sales Page Headlines
```text
Write 10 sales page headlines: each under 15 words, leading with a clear outcome, timeframe, or audience. Avoid vague adjectives and questions. Then pick the top 3 and briefly explain why they'll perform best.
```

---

## 02. Outcome-Based Value Proposition
```text
Write a value proposition built on three key outcomes. For each: state a specific result (with metric/timeframe), explain its impact in 2-3 sentences, and include one concrete example or recognisable scenario.
```

---

## 03. Relatable Problem Section
```text
Write a problem section (under 200 words) describing your audience's current situation and frustrations in their own words. Make it specific and relatable so they feel deeply understood, not lectured.
```

---

## 04. "This Is For You If" Section
```text
Write a "this is for you if" section with five specific, sentence-length statements describing your ideal client's situation, beliefs, and past attempts. Make each feel precise enough that the right reader instantly recognises themselves.
```

---

## 05. First-Person Positioning Statement
```text
Write a 70-90 word, first-person positioning statement: who you help (specific situation), the outcome you deliver (with metric/timeframe), how you do it (method), and why you (proof). Make it clear, specific, and instantly recognisable to your ideal client.
```

---

## 06. "This Is Not For You If" Section
```text
Write a "this is not for you if" section with four specific, sentence-length statements explaining who won't benefit and why. Focus on mindsets or situations, and keep the tone direct but respectful.
```
