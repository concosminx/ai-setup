# Work Productivity Prompts

A collection of AI prompts designed to boost work productivity, prioritize tasks, prepare for meetings, refine executive communication, optimize career growth, and drive high-leverage outcomes.

---

## Section 1: Task Management & Daily Productivity

### 01. Task Impact Ranking
Rank tasks based on real value and impact rather than artificial urgency.

```text
Here are my tasks: [list]. Rank by actual impact, not urgency. Tell me the 2 to do today and which to drop entirely.
```

---

### 02. Efficient Meeting Agenda
Create focused meeting agendas with a required decision outcome.

```text
Write the agenda for my [meeting type] with [person]. 4 items max. End with one decision I need to make.
```

---

### 03. Meeting Transcript Summary
Extract key outcomes, task owners, and open questions from meeting notes or transcripts.

```text
Summarize this meeting transcript: [paste]. What was decided, who owns what, what's still open.
```

---

### 04. Unblock Stuck Tasks
Identify hidden bottlenecks and root causes when feeling stuck.

```text
I've been stuck on [task] for 2 hours. Ask me 5 questions to figure out what's actually blocking me.
```

---

### 05. Outcome-Focused Weekly Update
Format weekly accomplishments for management, emphasizing results over tasks.

```text
Write my weekly update for my boss. Here's what I did: [list]. 3 bullets. Lead with outcomes, not activities.
```

---

### 06. High-Leverage Time Block
Maximize small windows of time before upcoming events with high-impact work.

```text
I have 45 minutes before [event]. What's the highest-leverage thing I can do right now? Don't suggest "organizing."
```

## Section 2: Executive Communication & Strategic Positioning

### 07. The Pre-Send Audit
Elevate the tone of an email to sound more senior and authoritative before sending.

```text
Read this email. Tell me what a senior person would assume about me from how I wrote it. Then rewrite it so they assume I'm three years more senior than I am: [paste]
```

---

### 08. The 30-Minute Meeting Prep
Fast-track meeting readiness when underprepared with high-value questions and risks.

```text
I have a meeting in 30 min about [topic]. I haven't prepped. Give me: 3 questions that make me sound like I read the doc, 1 risk to flag, and 1 thing to say if I get put on the spot.
```

---

### 09. The Recommendation in 4 Sentences
Deliver concise, unhedged decision recommendations with trade-offs.

```text
I need to recommend [A] over [B] to [person]. Write 4 sentences: the call, why now, what we lose by choosing A, why we accept that. No hedging words.
```

---

### 10. The Pushback That Doesn't Burn Capital
Respectfully push back on decisions while aligning with stakeholder goals.

```text
I think [decision] is wrong because [reason]. Write my pushback to [person] in a way that makes me sound like I'm protecting their goal, not opposing them. Keep it under 4 sentences.
```

---

### 11. The "What Did You Actually Do" Translator
Convert unstructured weekly notes into 3 high-impact, VP-level outcome bullets.

```text
Here's what I did this week: [paste rambling notes]. Rewrite it as 3 bullets a VP would skim and remember. Lead each with the business outcome, not what I worked on.
```

---

### 12. The Question That Gets You Invited Back
Ask insightful review questions exposing unstated assumptions and second-order effects.

```text
I'm reviewing [doc/proposal]. Give me 1 question that exposes the assumption no one is challenging, and 1 question that makes me sound like I'm thinking about second-order effects.
```

---

### 13. The Closer That Sets Up What's Next
Wrap up a completed project while naturally opening the door for the next opportunity.

```text
I just finished [project]. Write a 2-line wrap-up email to [manager] that closes this out and naturally surfaces that I'm ready for [next thing] — without being thirsty.
```

## Section 3: Career Growth & Job Application

### 14. The 6-Second Resume Rewriter
Act as a Google senior recruiter to rewrite resumes for instant 6-second scanning impact using the XYZ formula.

```text
Act as a senior recruiter at Google who screens resumes in under 6 seconds. Rewrite my resume to pass that filter:

* Create a 2-3 sentence professional summary with clear positioning and impact
* Rewrite all bullets using the XYZ formula: Accomplished X, measured by Y, by doing Z
* Add metrics to every achievement (%, $, scale)
* Replace weak verbs with strong action verbs
* Cut all filler and enforce a 1-page format (2 pages if 10+ years experience)
* Organize skills into clear categories aligned with the role
* Simplify education (degree, school, honors only)
* Remove red flags (gaps, outdated tools, irrelevant content)
* Improve visual hierarchy for fast scanning

Include:
* Full rewritten resume
* Before vs after for the 3 weakest bullets
* Summary of key improvements

Input: [Resume + Job Description]
```

---

### 15. The ATS Optimization Prompt
Optimize resume keywords, phrasing, and formatting for ATS platforms like Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever.

```text
Act as an ATS expert who understands systems like Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever. Optimize my resume to pass automated screening:

* Extract and integrate keywords from the job description
* Align formatting for ATS readability (no tables, clean structure)
* Improve keyword density without sounding unnatural
* Match skills, titles, and phrasing to the target role
* Ensure compatibility with parsing systems

Output:
* Optimized resume
* Keyword match breakdown
* ATS score estimate with improvements

Input: [Resume + Job Description]
```

---

### 16. The McKinsey Achievement Quantifier
Transform qualitative resume bullet points into McKinsey-style quantified achievement statements.

```text
Act as a resume strategist at McKinsey & Company. Turn every bullet into measurable impact:

* Add metrics: revenue, cost savings, growth, time saved, scale
* Quantify leadership, process creation, and improvements
* Use the formula: Achieved X -> Result Y -> By doing Z
* Highlight promotions, ownership, and outcomes

Output:
* Before vs after for every bullet
* Fully quantified achievement statements

Input: Your current bullet points
```

---

### 17. The High-Conversion Cover Letter
Generate a tailored 250-300 word cover letter with strong company context and 90-day contribution pitch.

```text
Act as a recruitment director at Robert Half. Write a 250-300 word cover letter that gets interviews:

* Strong opening tied to company context (no generic intro)
* Show research (product, strategy, or recent move)
* Match 3 key skills to job requirements
* Include one quantified achievement
* Demonstrate cultural fit naturally
* Suggest a relevant contribution in the first 90 days
* Close confidently with a clear next step

Output:
* Ready-to-send cover letter
* Mark sections that should be customized per company

Input: [Job description + 3 achievements + interest in company]
```
