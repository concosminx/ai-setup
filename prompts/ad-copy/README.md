# Ad Copy Prompts

A collection of AI prompts designed for crafting high-performing Facebook/Instagram feed ads, retargeting copy, 30-second YouTube pre-roll scripts, and scroll-stopping ad hooks.

---

## 01. Lead Magnet Ad Variants (FB/IG)
```text
Write three ad variants for your lead magnet: each includes a scroll-stopping hook, 2–3 sentences explaining the value, and a clear CTA. Keep body copy under 100 words, specific to your audience and problem, with no questions or exclamation marks.
```

---

## 02. Cold-Audience Feed Ads
```text
Write three cold-audience ad variants: open with a situation or result-driven hook, establish credibility with a specific proof point, and close with a clear CTA. Tailor each to your audience and offer outcome. Under 120 words each. Write for Facebook and Instagram feed placement.
```

---

## 03. Warm Prospect Retargeting Ads
```text
Write two retargeting ads (under 100 words each) for warm prospects: address a key hesitation directly, include one specific proof point, and close with a clear CTA back to the sales page or a call.
```

---

## 04. 30-Second YouTube Pre-Roll Script
```text
Write a 30-second YouTube pre-roll script: hook (5s), problem (8s), solution (10s), CTA (7s). Keep it conversational, with a clear instruction, and include brief visual notes for each section.
```

---

## 05. 10 Varied Scroll-Stopping Ad Hooks
```text
Write 10 ad hooks (1–2 sentences each) using varied angles, results, observations, problems, and audience-specific statements. Then select the top 3 and briefly explain why they're most likely to perform.
```

---

## 06. Live Event & Training Promotion Ads
```text
Write three ad variants (under 110 words each) promoting your free live training: open with the problem or outcome, describe the training in two sentences, and close with a clear CTA including date and time.
```
