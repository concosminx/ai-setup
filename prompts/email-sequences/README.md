# Email Sequences Prompts

A collection of AI prompts designed for crafting value-driven welcome series, story-based nurture emails, case study emails, and objection-handling email sequences.

---

## 01. First Welcome & Delivery Email
```text
Write a first welcome email (under 220 words): include the download link, one line on how to use it, and a brief credibility-based intro. Keep it personal, not generic, and avoid "Welcome" or "Congratulations." Include three subject lines.
```

---

## 02. Second Welcome Email (Story & Insight)
```text
Write a second welcome email (under 250 words): share a specific result as a short story, highlight one actionable lesson, and tease the next email. No selling. Include three subject lines.
```

---

## 03. Third Welcome Email (Trust Building)
```text
Write a third welcome email (220–280 words): share one genuinely useful insight with no selling, build trust through value, and close with a line that shows you understand the reader. Include three subject lines.
```

---

## 04. Fourth Welcome Email (Soft Pitch & CTA)
```text
Write a fourth welcome email (under 260 words): introduce your paid offer as a natural next step, include 1 - 2 lines of proof, keep the tone soft, and add a clear CTA. Include three subject lines.
```

---

## 05. Client Case Study Email
```text
Write a 240–300 word client case study email: open with the result, share the before and what you did, and close with a reflective line and soft CTA. Keep it story-driven. Include three subject lines.
```

---

## 06. Key Objection Handling Email
```text
Write a 220–270 word email addressing a key objection: acknowledge it honestly, respond with a specific example or reframe, and close with a soft CTA. Include three subject lines.
```
