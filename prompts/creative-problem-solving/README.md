# Creative Problem Solving Prompts

A collection of AI prompts designed to break mental blocks, generate unexpected solutions, challenge assumptions, spark creative brainstorming, and build cohesive brand & design systems.

---

## Section 1: Ideation & Problem Solving

### 01. Unexpected Angles
Break through conventional thinking with unconventional perspectives.

```text
I'm stuck on [problem]. Give me 5 weird, unexpected angles most people wouldn't consider. No safe answers.
```

---

### 02. Devil's Advocate & Blind Spots
Stress-test ideas by identifying potential failure points and overconfidence.

```text
Play devil's advocate on [my idea/plan]. Where could this fail? Assume I'm overconfident.
```

---

### 03. Workaround Spectrum
Explore a wide range of solutions from pragmatic to wild, ranked by feasibility.

```text
I want to [goal] but [obstacle]. List 10 workarounds, from obvious to ridiculous. Rank by feasibility.
```

---

### 04. Expert Persona Roleplay
Adopt specialized expert mindsets to approach a challenge from fresh viewpoints.

```text
Pretend you're [expert type]. How would they approach [my problem]? What would they do first?
```

---

### 05. Thoughtful Gift Brainstorming
Generate creative and personalized gift ideas avoiding generic cliches.

```text
Brainstorm gift ideas for [person]. Here's what I know about them: [details]. No gift cards, no flowers.
```

---

### 06. Multi-Style Naming Generator
Generate varied naming ideas categorized by brand tone with quick explanations.

```text
I need a name for [thing]. Give me 20 options across 4 styles: playful, professional, weird, simple. Explain each.
```

---

## Section 2: Visual Identity & Design Systems

### 07. Brief Decoder
Break down client briefs into clear design directions extracting objectives, target audience, visual tone, and key deliverables.

```text
Act as a senior creative director. Break down this client brief into a clear design direction. Pull out the objective, audience, key message, visual tone, and deliverables: [paste brief].
```

---

### 08. Logo Idea Machine
Generate 10 distinct, symbolic logo concepts with clear design rationale and story.

```text
Act as a logo design specialist. Generate 10 distinct logo concepts for a [industry] brand. Base each on clear symbolism and meaning. Explain the thinking behind every idea.
```

---

### 09. Layout Architect
Create 5 structural layout directions for posters, social posts, or web sections focused on hierarchy, spacing, flow, and balance.

```text
Generate 5 layout directions for a [poster, social post, website section]. Describe each one in terms of hierarchy, spacing, flow, and balance.
```

---

### 10. Type System Builder
Design complete typography systems with font pairings, hierarchy scales, and usage guidelines.

```text
Act as a typography specialist. Build a type system for a [industry] brand. Cover font pairings, hierarchy levels, and clear rules for usage.
```

---

### 11. Design Auditor
Get objective design feedback on composition, typography, color harmony, and brand alignment.

```text
Act as a professional design critic. Review this design and give honest feedback on composition, type, color, and brand alignment. Tell me what to fix: [describe your design].
```

---

### 12. Brand System Creator
Build comprehensive visual identity systems covering logo direction, color rationale, typography, and aesthetic rules.

```text
Act as a brand strategist. Build a complete visual identity system for a [industry] brand. Cover logo direction, color rationale, typography, and overall aesthetic.
```
