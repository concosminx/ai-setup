# Brand Strategy Prompts

A collection of AI prompts designed for market research, ideal customer profile (ICP) language libraries, content pillars, writing voice profiles, and positioning differentiators.

---

## 01. Detailed Target Audience Research Profile
```text
Act as a market research expert. For my defined audience, create a detailed profile: top 10 problems, exact language they use, current solutions, what's failing, and their emotional state. Keep it specific to this stage, there should be no generic founder insights.
```

---

## 02. ICP Language Library
```text
Using the audience analysis, build a 3-part language library for my ICP: 1. Pain vocabulary: how they describe problems. 2. Aspirational language: how they define success. 3. Self-framing: how they describe their expertise. Keep phrasing raw, specific, and true to how they actually speak.
```

---

## 03. 3–5 Distinct Belief-Anchored Content Pillars
```text
Help me develop three to five content pillars. Each pillar must be anchored to a specific belief or observation rather than a broad topic area. Each pillar must be distinct enough that a post from one could not plausibly sit within another. Each pillar must connect to the business outcomes my audience cares about.
```

---

## 04. Brand Voice Profile & Replicable Guidelines
```text
Analyse my writing samples and build a voice profile: sentence structure, vocabulary, tone, openings/closings, and use of examples or stories. Then turn this into clear second-person guidelines so another writer can replicate my style accurately.
```

---

## 05. 70–90 Word Positioning Statement
```text
Write a 70–90 word, first-person positioning statement: who you help (specific situation), the outcome you deliver (with metric/timeframe), how you do it (method), and why you (proof). Make it clear, specific, and instantly recognisable to your ideal client.
```

---

## 06. Offer Differentiators Mapping
```text
Define your offer, audience, and competitors. Then map key differentiators: 1. Your unique method. 2. Specific results achieved Inc numbers. 3. The niche you serve. For each, write 2 - 3 clear, specific sentences, no vague claims.
```
